The problem

Health information is still scattered.

An X-ray in a drawer. Results in a WhatsApp photo. Zulu brings appointments, records and results into one connected record that stays accessible across the network.

01 · Appointments

Book and manage appointments.

View availability, book, reschedule or cancel, and keep the doctor, specialty, time and location attached to each visit.

02 · Family health

Look after your family's health from one place.

Link separate profiles for parents, children and other family members. Manage their appointments and records from Lebanon or abroad.

03 · Records & history

Keep your medical history organized.

Diagnoses, prescriptions, imaging, allergies and vitals are organized in one record. Records from connected providers can be added automatically.

04 · Laboratory & imaging results

Receive results on your phone.

When a connected laboratory or imaging center releases a result, it is added to the record and organized by date and test. Clinical interpretation remains with your doctor.

Security

Designed for health information.

Zulu applies access controls and security measures across the storage and transmission of health information.

Not medical advice

Information, not diagnosis.

Zulu organizes health information. It does not diagnose or replace clinical judgment.
